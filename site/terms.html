Before you use it

  • While CapsJump runs, Caps Lock becomes a shortcut key and no longer types capitals. Quit CapsJump and Caps Lock works normally again. If it ever crashes, Caps Lock does nothing until CapsJump starts again or the Mac restarts.
  • CapsJump moves windows and switches tabs only when you press one of your keys.
  • There's no account, no payment and nothing to sign up for.
